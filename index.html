Questions you might reasonably have

What is SendMeOneDollar.org?

It's a website that asks you to send one dollar. That is the entire concept. No product ships, no newsletter arrives, no upsell appears later. You send one dollar to a stranger on the internet, and the stranger is genuinely grateful. It is the simplest possible transaction.

Why should I send one dollar?

Because it's one dollar. You won't feel it leave your account, and yet — one dollar at a time — it adds up to something genuinely encouraging. Think of it as the cheapest way to quietly make a stranger's day.

Is it safe to send money here?

Yes. Every payment is handled entirely by PayPal's secure checkout — this site never sees or stores your card or bank details. You're simply clicking a button that hands you over to PayPal. More detail lives in the privacy policy.

Can I pay by card without a PayPal account?

Yes. Use the "Debit or Credit Card" button and you can send your dollar with a card directly — no PayPal account required.

Where does my dollar actually go?

To me — the person who built this website. It will most likely be spent on something worth approximately one dollar, continuing the ancient cycle of commerce. You are part of something now.

Can I send more than a dollar?

You can. The site is named after one dollar out of modesty, not law — PayPal will happily let you change the amount. No pressure, though. One dollar is, and always will be, plenty.

The $1 bounty

Get five people to send me a dollar and I'll send you one. That's a 20% commission, which is frankly generous.

Your PayPal email is only for sending you your dollar. It never appears on this site. Every payment through your link is checked with PayPal and counted, and once you hit five I'll send you a dollar. You don't need to do anything. My decision is final.
