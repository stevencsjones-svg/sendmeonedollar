Privacy Policy

Short version: this site barely knows you exist. Here's the longer version, which is also short.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to count visitors. It collects anonymous, aggregated usage data and sets cookies to do so. The site owner sees numbers go up and to the right — never your name, email, or anything that identifies you personally.

Payments

The button sends you to PayPal.Me. Your entire payment happens on PayPal's website under their terms and privacy policy. This site never sees, touches, or stores your card, bank, or payment details. It just points you at PayPal and steps aside.

Sharing your data

None of it is sold, rented, or shared with third parties. There's almost nothing to share, and even if there were, I wouldn't. That would be a lot of effort for a website about one dollar.
